# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

(React Native / Expo app for iPhone and Android. One shared Makinasal brand identity; native affordances such as safe areas, back gesture and system pickers follow each OS.)

## Users
Buyers and sellers of construction machinery, trucks, spare parts and tyres in Turkey, on phones, often on site or on the road: individual sellers and operators as well as companies and fleet owners. Individual and corporate users carry equal weight. Turkish is the primary language, English is supported.

## Product Purpose
Makinasal is a sahibinden.com-style marketplace for construction machines, trucks and similar vehicles plus their parts and tyres, extended with a "work network" (rentals, operators and technicians, job requests, part requests, quotes, company profiles and inventories). The mobile app brings the website's features to phones: search, listing, messaging, offers, favorites, notifications, trust scores and quotes.

## Positioning
A trade-specific marketplace and work network for heavy equipment, with spare-part number search, trust scores that both sides must confirm, and a private offer/quote flow, rather than a generic classifieds site.

## Operating Context
Used in short sessions: search, compare, call or message the seller, send an offer. Listings carry photos, price, year, hours or kilometres, province and seller trust level. Messages block IBAN and external links and warn about deposits (kapora). New listings are moderated before going live. Payments are not collected yet.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Expo SDK 58, Expo Router, Supabase backend shared with the Next.js website. Brand palette is fixed and must not change: navy #25272D, burgundy #800020, warm paper background #F6F5F3, border #DDD9DC, muted #66616A. Product copy is Turkish with an English translation layer (lib/i18n).

## Brand Commitments
Name "Makinasal". Existing palette (above) is binding. Trust, verification and "no deposit before seeing the item" messaging is part of the product.

## Evidence on Hand
Real listings, sellers and reviews come from the live database. No photography, logo artwork or testimonials are supplied beyond what users upload.

## Product Principles
- Trust is visible: verification, trust level and scam warnings are never decoration.
- Speed in the field beats ornament: the core path (search, open, contact) stays short.
- The same Makinasal identity on iPhone and Android.
- Turkish first, never broken in English.
