# Surface brief: Makinasal mobile app (all screens)

Scope and mode: Operate. Whole-app visual replacement inside the fixed Makinasal palette (navy #25272D, burgundy #800020, paper #F6F5F3, line #DDD9DC, muted #66616A).

Audience and job: buyers, sellers, operators and companies finding machines, parts and work on a phone, often outdoors; scan fast, compare, contact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Listings and records read as filled-in registration forms: every fact lives in a labelled field, so numbers and names can be compared at a glance. Refuses the rounded soft-card list with a bold title and grey meta line.

OWN-WORLD: The Turkish vehicle registration / tescil document. Tiny condensed uppercase field labels over larger values, hairline cell borders that share edges, 4px corners, no shadows, tabular figures, section headers as thin ruled bands. Barlow Condensed for labels, Barlow for values and copy. Palette unchanged.

STORY: The visitor sees MARKA, MODEL YILI, SAAT, İL as fields before reading prose, trusts the structure, and contacts the seller or makes an offer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy header with wordmark, search field with suggestions, then a ruled grid of eight destinations (icon, label), then the first listings as photo plus a three-cell field strip (YIL, KULLANIM, İL) and a price cell. Primary action (search) sits in the hero.

FORM: Registration-document fields (position 5 of 7 grounded candidates: data plate, classified columns, road sign, drawing sheet, registration document, tyre sidewall, site signage); seed: seed:729d494e32b4618f.
